A demo for Snouty And The Great Rift is available on Steam now!

Snouty And The Great Rift is an open-world adventure in which you play as Snouty, a humble piglet thrust into a whimsical tale of magic and mystery. I conceptualized and created this game entirely on my own, a process that taught me about 2D/3D art, Unity programming, debugging and optimisation, and many other skills besides. Development is still ongoing, but I am incredibly proud of what I have created so far.

The game is built in Unity 6 (URP), and I used Blender and Procreate for art. Since the project was already very ambitious, I wanted to stick to a workflow I was comfortable with and make the best game I possibly could. I also used a Trello board to keep myself on-task.
