My current role as a Frontend Developer has seen me partner with Rare, a legendary studio whose games such as Goldeneye and Banjo-Kazooie have resonated with me since childhood. It has been fantastic to work on Sea Of Thieves and craft exciting new UI for the game's ongoing updates. Most notably, I have worked on seasonal updates and many aspects of the Custom Seas feature set.

The game's UI uses a Web-based event-oriented architecture to overlay screens on top of gameplay in the Unreal project. It is an unusual system that has allowed me to get to grips with lots of custom tooling and code, and proven that I am able to adapt to the needs of any project.
